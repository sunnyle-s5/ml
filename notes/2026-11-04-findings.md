## 1 · The live Popular list changed in October

Source: `rank_<cluster>` tables in `~/ml/data/<market>.duckdb` (copy of the site's live ranking scores), opened read-only.

- **Formula.** Until 21 Oct (BR/PE/CO) / 22 Oct (MX, TH, PH, BD, PK) the stored score was exactly the raw
  `(3000·bet + 7000·players) / 10000`. From the next day on, on all 20 clusters with a ranking table:
  `score ≈ 100 × (0.7 · players / Σ players in the brand + 0.3 · bet / Σ bet in the brand)`
  (share-of-total normalisation; within 0.001 on 88 % of mxk1 rows, 97 % of mx96g1 rows since 22 Oct).
  The Sep ask "normalise 30/70" has shipped, with a different normalisation than ours (we tested divide-by-max
  and percentile rank, not share of total).
- **Displayed order (MX).** `rank` (what the site shows) followed the raw formula until 13 Oct (83–91 % of the
  top 10 shared with `calcRank`). On 14 Oct it was replaced by a fixed list (35–47 % overlap with the raw
  formula), frozen through 23 Oct. That list shares 83 % of its top 10 with the new formula's first day (22 Oct), and the
  displayed list has followed the new formula since (83–91 %). → explains MX "live as stored" HR@10 35.5 % (13–19 Oct)
  → 64.4 % (20–26 Oct). Not checked yet: whether other markets had the same 14 Oct fixed list.
- **Sunday bug NOT fixed.** mx96g1: the ranking's 7-day bet input / our own rounds = 0.87–0.92 on every day from
  14 Sep to 2 Nov, before and after 22 Oct (a fully counted week would be ~1.0; missing ~80 % of the Sunday ≈ −11 %).
